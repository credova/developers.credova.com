# Save Bank Accounts

A saved bank account has an `id` that starts with `ba_`. You use it to create ACH payments and payouts. Save a bank account in one of two ways:

- **[Credova Elements](#save-a-bank-account-with-credova-elements)** captures the bank account in your web app, by manual entry or instant verification. Account numbers never touch your servers.
- **[The Direct API](#save-a-bank-account-from-your-servers)** sends the bank account from your servers.

## Request Account Setting Access

Each way to collect a bank account needs an account setting. Request the setting for the option you plan to use:

- **Manual Entry** needs the `payment-methods:bank-accounts:manual-creation` account setting type. In the merchant portal, it is the `Manual Bank Account Creation` setting.
- **Instant Verification** needs the `payment-methods:bank-accounts:verification` account setting type. In the merchant portal, it is the `Bank Account Verification` setting.

To request a setting, go to the [merchant portal](https://portal.publicsquare.com/settings/features) and click the `Request this feature` button next to it.
Alternatively, you can request access via the [API](/api/accounts/request-account-setting-access).

Your account needs the setting before manual bank account creation or instant bank account verification works.

The `payment-methods:bank-accounts:manual-creation` setting also lets you send bank account details when you [process an ACH payment](/guides/payments/accept-bank-account-payments#create-with-bank-account-details) or [an ACH payout](/guides/payouts/process-ach-payouts#create-with-bank-account-details). You do not need to create the bank account first.

## Save a Bank Account with Credova Elements

Credova Elements gives you two ways to collect a bank account:

- [Manual Entry](#manual-entry): your customer types the routing number and account number into the Bank Account Elements.
- [Instant Verification](#instant-verification): your customer connects their bank account and Credova verifies it instantly.

With either option, you create a `bank account` that holds the non-sensitive information about the bank account. You can safely store the bank account's `id` value in your database to link it with the appropriate payment or customer association.

When you create the bank account, you can optionally pass in a `customer_id` to associate with an existing [Created Customer](/api/accounts/create-customer) and `billing_details` to remove the requirement to pass them when [Creating a Payment](/api/financial/create-payment).

#### Storing Bank Accounts

Now that you are capturing bank account data in your web application, it is time to store the bank account in your account.

To do this, we will call the `create bank account` method from the SDK, passing the Bank Account Elements as data points in the payload.

Add a submit function along with a button to trigger it:

<Tabs className="publicsquare-tabs" queryString="sdk">
  <TabItem value="javascript" label="JavaScript">
```javascript showLineNumbers title=index.js
async function submit (e: FormEvent<HTMLFormElement>,) {
  try {
    const formData = new FormData(e.currentTarget)
    const account_holder_name = formData.get('accountHolderName');
    const routing_number = bankAccountElement.routingNumber.el.value
    const account_number = bankAccountElement.accountNumber.el.value
    // highlight-start
    const bankAccount = await publicsquare.bankAccounts.create({
      account_holder_name,
      routing_number,
      account_number,
    });
    // highlight-end
    console.log(bankAccount); // e.g { id: 'ba_1234567890', ... }
  } catch (error) {
    console.error(error);
  }
}
```

### Instant Verification

Instant verification lets your customer connect their bank account so that Credova can verify it instantly, then stores the verified bank account in your account.

#### Adding Bank Account Verification Elements

Credova has partnered with [Flinks](https://www.flinks.com/) to provide a secure way to collect bank accounts.
Once installed and configured, add the Bank Account Verification Elements to your application.

#### Nightly Balance Refresh

We automatically refresh the balance for all verified bank accounts for up to 30 days after verification. 
To obtain the most recent balance, you can make a call to the [Get Bank Account Balance](/api/financial/get-bank-account-balance-by-id) endpoint.

If more than 30 days have passed since the account was verified, you must re-verify the bank account to update its balance.

<Alert type="warning">Bank accounts with MFA enabled do not support nightly balance refresh.</Alert>

## Save a Bank Account from Your Servers

<Alert type="warning">Your servers receive and send bank account numbers in this flow. To keep them off your servers, use [Credova Elements](#save-a-bank-account-with-credova-elements).</Alert>

A restricted key needs the `payment-method:write` or `payment-method:capture` permission. Your account also needs the `payment-methods:bank-accounts:manual-creation` account setting from [Request Account Setting Access](#request-account-setting-access).

### Save a Bank Account

Send the bank account in a `POST` request to [Create Bank Account](/api/financial/create-bank-account):

```shell showLineNumbers title="Save a Bank Account"
curl 'https://api.publicsquare.com/payment-methods/bank-accounts' \
-X 'POST' \
-H 'X-API-KEY: <SECRET_API_KEY>' \
-H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
-d '{
  "account_holder_name": "John Doe",
  "account_holder_type": "individual",
  "account_type": "checking",
  "routing_number": "110000000",
  "account_number": "000123456789",
  "customer_id": "cus_7Ay5mcUXAxwrN6wQEQUVEHBCJ"
}'
```

The request body accepts these fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `account_holder_name` | Name on the bank account. |
| `account_holder_type` | `individual` or `company`. |
| `account_type` | `checking` or `savings`. |
| `routing_number` | Routing number. |
| `account_number` | Account number. |
| `customer_id` | ID of an existing customer to associate with the bank account. |
| `billing_details` | Billing address for the bank account. |

Send `account_holder_name` and `account_holder_type`. Some processors require them.
