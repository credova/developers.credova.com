# Getting Started

Credova provides APIs, SDKs and eCommerce plugins to accept payments, send payouts and manage transactions. Complete these steps once, then follow the guide for your integration path.

## Create an account

Create an account in the Credova Portal, or sign in to an existing account.

<AuthButtons />

## Get your API keys

Open [API keys](https://portal.publicsquare.com/developers/api-keys) in the Portal and click `Reveal` to copy a key.

| Key | Where you use it | Keep it secret |
|-----|------------------|----------------|
| `Publishable Key` | In the browser, to load Credova Elements. | No |
| `Secret Key` | On your server, to call the Credova API. | Yes |

Each account has `Test` and `Production` keys. Use your `Test` keys while you build, because they never move money. To limit what a key can do, create a restricted key. See [API Keys](/concepts/api-keys) for permissions.

## Choose what you build

### Payments

Pick how your customers enter their payment details.

| Integration | Choose it when | Card data on your servers | Start with |
|-------------|----------------|---------------------------|------------|
| Credova Elements | Customers pay in your web app. | No. Credova Elements collects it. | [Accept Card Payments](/guides/payments/elements/accept-card-payments) |
| Direct API | Your organization is PCI certified and your servers already have the card. | Yes | [Create a Payment with Card Details](/guides/payments/direct-api/payment-with-card-details) |
| eCommerce plugins | You sell on Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento or BigCommerce. | No. The plugin collects it. | [eCommerce Plugins](/plugins) |

Credova Elements also accepts [bank accounts](/guides/payments/elements/accept-bank-account-payments), [Apple Pay](/guides/payments/elements/accept-apple-pay) and [Google Pay](/guides/payments/elements/accept-google-pay).

The Direct API can also [save a card](/guides/payments/direct-api/save-cards) or [save a bank account](/guides/payments/direct-api/save-bank-accounts) for later payments.

Every integration creates the same payment, so the same options and follow-up guides apply. When you create the payment, you can [authorize now and capture later](/guides/payments/authorize-and-capture-payments), [verify the card first](/guides/payments/verify-cards), or add [3D Secure](/concepts/3d-secure) with Credova Elements.

After the payment:

- [Cancel Payments](/guides/payments/cancel-payments)
- [Refund Payments](/guides/refunds/refund-payments)
- [Transactions and Settlements](/guides/transactions/search-and-view-transactions)
- [Webhooks](/concepts/webhooks)

### Payouts

Send money to cards and bank accounts. Start with [Send Card Payouts](/guides/payouts/process-card-payouts) or [Send Bank Account Payouts](/guides/payouts/process-ach-payouts), and [cancel a payout](/guides/payouts/cancel-payouts) before it settles. If your organization is PCI certified, you can also send the card details in the payout request with [Create with Card Details](/guides/payouts/process-card-payouts#create-with-card-details).

### Marketplaces

Onboard sellers and move funds to and from them. Start with [Onboard Sellers](/guides/marketplaces/onboard-sellers).

## Go live

[Test your integration](/api/testing) with test cards, then work through the [production checklist](/guides/production-checklist). Replace your `Test` keys with `Production` keys when you go live.
