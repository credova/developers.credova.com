### 1. Set up your Account

Head over to the [Credova Portal](https://portal.publicsquare.com).

<AuthButtons />

### 2. Choose your Integration Path

Pick the path that matches where your customers enter their payment details:

| Path | Choose it when | Card data on your servers |
|------|----------------|---------------------------|
| [Browser](#accept-payments-in-the-browser) | Customers enter payment details in your web app. | No. Credova Elements collects it. |
| [Server-to-server](#accept-payments-server-to-server) | Your organization is PCI certified and your servers already have the card. | Yes |
| [eCommerce plugins](/plugins) | You sell on Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento or BigCommerce. | No |
| [Marketplaces](/guides/marketplaces/onboard-sellers) | You onboard sellers and move funds to and from them. | Depends on how you collect payments |
